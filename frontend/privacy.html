Privacy notice

Last updated 27 September 2026

Eval Workbench is in private beta. This notice explains what we collect, why, where it is stored, and how to get it deleted. Questions or requests: productlab.support@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Access requests. Your email address, and the name or team and description you choose to give. We use these only to review your request and to send you your API key.
  • Data your application sends. If you connect an app, it sends us the inputs, outputs, test cases, timings and traces you choose to send, through our SDK or API. For live traffic this can include identifiers you choose for your end users and their conversations, and tags and metadata you attach. When someone on your team reviews a result, we store the name they enter and their note with the decision. We store them to show you your results. We do not use them for anything else, we do not train models on them, and we do not sell or share them.
  • API keys. We store only a one-way hash of each key, so we cannot recover your key.
  • Request logs. Our hosting provider records standard web server logs (IP address, time, path, status) for security and troubleshooting. We also use your IP address briefly, in memory, to limit repeated requests.

Don't send passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information about your users through the SDK. If your test data or traffic contains personal data, you are responsible for having the right to send it to us.

What stays in your browser

The demo data, and the API address and key you connect with, are saved in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to us except when your browser uses the key to load your own data. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Who processes it

  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run, United States) runs the service and keeps its request logs.
  • Neon hosts the database where access requests and project data are stored.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces for the web app, so your browser requests them from Google.

Eval Workbench never calls an AI model on your behalf. Your application calls its own model provider with its own key.

How long we keep it

Project data is kept until you delete it or the beta ends. Access requests are kept until your project is deleted, or until you ask us to remove a request that was not approved. Request logs are kept for the hosting provider's standard period.

Deleting your data

  • In the web app, connect with your key, open Settings and choose Delete my project. This permanently removes every suite, run, result and trace, all your keys, and your access request with your email.
  • From code: client.delete_project("<your project id>").
  • Or email productlab.support@gmail.com and we will delete it for you, including a request that was never approved.

Deleted data can't be recovered. The database provider's short-term recovery copies expire on their own within their retention window.

Changes

If this notice changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, email beta users.
